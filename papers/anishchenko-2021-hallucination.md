# De novo protein design by deep network hallucination

The paper that **inverted a structure predictor into a generator**. Random sequences are optimized until the network's own predictions become confident, and the resulting sequences fold — establishing hallucination as a design method and founding the lineage that runs through [[wang-2022-functional-site-scaffolding]] and [[wicky-2022-symmetric-assembly-design]] into conditional diffusion.

**Problem.** Deep learning had already been applied to proteins in pieces: language-model architectures generating sequences without explicit structure, methods generating backbones without sequence, and inverse-folding methods fitting sequences onto specified backbones. None addressed the **classical de novo design problem of simultaneously generating a new backbone structure and a sequence that encodes it**. The authors' opening observation is that trRosetta could accurately predict structures of de novo designed proteins from a *single* sequence, in the complete absence of co-evolution information, and predicted substitution effects consistent with biophysical expectation — suggesting the network had learned fundamental sequence–structure relationships rather than merely exploiting co-evolution, and might therefore be invertible.

**Approach.** Start from a **random amino acid sequence**, whose predicted inter-residue distance and orientation maps are diffuse and featureless. Then run **Monte Carlo simulated annealing in sequence space**: at each step substitute a random amino acid at a random position, re-predict the distance and orientation distributions with **trRosetta**, and accept or reject by the Metropolis criterion on the change in **Kullback–Leibler divergence** between the predicted distributions *P* and generic length- and separation-dependent **background** distributions *Q*, summed over all residue pairs. The objective is therefore **contrast** — sharpness of the predicted maps — with no target structure specified anywhere. Final sharpened maps are converted to 3D coordinates by direct minimization with trRosetta. The explicit analogy the authors draw is to **DeepDream**: inverting a network trained to recognize patterns so that it generates them.

**Key results.**

- After roughly **20,000 Monte Carlo steps**, predicted distance maps were at least as featured as those for naturally occurring sequences and for structurally confirmed Rosetta de novo designs; **2,000** proteins were generated this way.
- Outputs are **novel**: median BLAST E-value of the closest UniRef90 hit is **0.17**, and TM-align scores to the closest PDB match average **0.78** (range roughly 0.6–0.9) — recognizably protein-like but not memorized.
- Structures span **all-α, all-β and mixed α/β** classes, with **95 distinct sub-folds** at a 0.75 TM-score clustering threshold; the 129 experimentally tested designs fall into **27 structural clusters**.
- The hallucinated backbones **resemble idealized de novo designed proteins more than native proteins** — regular secondary structure, short loops, none of the idiosyncrasies of natives — despite the network having been trained only on natives. The closest PDB structure to design 0738_mod is the de novo designed **Top7**.
- **Orthogonal validation by Rosetta**, which the network never saw: for **82 of 129** proteins the lowest-energy structure from extended-chain folding simulations was within **3.0 Å Cα RMSD** of the hallucinated structure, and for **all 129** the lowest-energy structure starting from the design model beat anything found from an extended chain.
- **Experimentally: 27 of 129** expressed proteins gave SEC peaks corresponding to monomeric or small oligomeric species, all with CD spectra consistent with the target structures; **21** were highly thermostable with apparent T_m above **70 °C**, and the α/β designs showed no unfolding transition up to 95 °C.
- **Three structures solved.** Design 0515 by solution NMR — a monomeric antiparallel four-helix bundle, ensemble Cα RMSD **1.82 Å** over 100 residues to the hallucinated model. Design 0217 by X-ray at **2.9 Å** — a three-helix bundle, **2.53 Å** backbone RMSD over 100 residues. Design 0738_mod by X-ray at **2.4 Å** — **3.68 Å** Cα RMSD over 96 residues overall, but with the N- and C-terminal halves aligning at **1.32 Å over 57 residues** and **2.17 Å over 43 residues** respectively, with many side-chain rotamers recovered despite the network operating only at the backbone level. Closest BLAST hits in the PDB for those halves have E-values of 0.29 and 0.63, so the accuracy is not memorization.

**The oligomerization finding.** Directly relevant to [[symmetric-oligomer-design]] and worth stating separately: **many of the hallucinated proteins form oligomers in solution**, despite every sequence having been modelled as a monomer. Design 0217 forms a **dimer in the crystal structure**, corroborated by SEC-MALS and NMR rotational correlation time; design 0417 showed transient self-association that ultimately prevented NMR structure determination. The authors' diagnosis is that **trRosetta was trained on native structures including many homo- and hetero-oligomers, so the model did not learn to disfavour surface hydrophobic residues to the degree needed for soluble monomers** — the network appears to have absorbed the sequence features of native protein–protein interfaces. They note this hit β-sandwich designs particularly hard, plausibly reflecting immunoglobulin-fold interdomain interactions in the training set. The fix is straightforward — **substituting surface hydrophobic residues with polar ones converted several oligomers to monomers**, and one such variant (0738_mod) is what crystallized. A second, distinct limitation is that trRosetta is **inherently low-resolution**, with side chains and packing not represented explicitly, producing core overpacking at most of the positions where models and experimental structures disagree.

**Significance.** Three things descend from this paper. (1) **Hallucination as a method** — the loss-function-driven inversion of a predictor, which [[wang-2022-functional-site-scaffolding]] extends by adding motif constraints and [[wicky-2022-symmetric-assembly-design]] extends by adding a cyclic-symmetry term, before [[watson-2023-rfdiffusion]] absorbs both as conditional-diffusion tasks. The authors anticipate exactly this, noting the approach can be extended to RoseTTAFold and AlphaFold2 and that the loss can be generalized to include binding motifs or catalytic sites. (2) **The design-without-a-blueprint argument** — unlike traditional design, where scaffold topology and secondary-structure element lengths are specified in advance, the network hallucinates plausible structures from scratch, so building a scaffold around a functional site needs no pre-specified map. (3) **The failure mode that recurs** — optimizing against a network yields sequences with aberrant surface properties, here diagnosed as learned interface features and exposed hydrophobics. That is the same family of problem [[wicky-2022-symmetric-assembly-design]] later hit at much greater severity and solved with [[dauparas-2022-proteinmpnn]] rather than by hand.

**Connections.**

- Founds the hallucination lineage: → [[wang-2022-functional-site-scaffolding]] (motif constraints), → [[wicky-2022-symmetric-assembly-design]] (symmetry constraints), → generalized by [[watson-2023-rfdiffusion]], → and revived with gradients by [[frank-2024-relaxed-sequence-optimization]], which implements the gradient-based optimization this paper proposed in its closing paragraphs.
- Uses **trRosetta**, the predecessor of [[rosettafold]]; the authors explicitly propose porting the method to RoseTTAFold and to [[jumper-2021-alphafold2]], which is what [[wicky-2022-symmetric-assembly-design]] does.
- Its uncontrolled homo-oligomerization is the accidental precursor to the *deliberate* symmetric assemblies of [[symmetric-oligomer-design]].
- Concepts: [[diffusion-generative-design]] (as the pre-diffusion generative route), [[motif-scaffolding]], [[symmetric-assembly-design]].
