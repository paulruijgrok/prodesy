# Chroma

Programmable diffusion generative model for proteins and complexes from Generate Biomedicines. Introduced in [[ingraham-2023-chroma]].

**Distinctive features.** A diffusion prior that respects polymer-ensemble conformational statistics (correlated noise), sub-quadratic architecture for long-range reasoning, and Bayesian conditioning on symmetry, substructure, shape, semantics and natural-language prompts. 310 designs experimentally validated; two crystal structures match at ~ångström backbone RMSD.

**Context.** Independent counterpart to [[rfdiffusion]] within [[diffusion-generative-design]]; different lineage, similar success — evidence the paradigm is robust.
