# Overview — Deep learning for protein design

Evolving synthesis of the Prodesy wiki. Current scope: the deep-learning core (batch 1), the generative-design expansion (batch 2), the symmetric-assembly history (batch 3), repeat/parametric design plus relaxed-sequence hallucination (batch 4), binder design (batch 5), the Genie lineage (batch 6), and enzyme design (batch 7). Will broaden as more of `sources/` is ingested.

## The through-line

A single pipeline now organizes the field:

> **generate a backbone → design its sequence → predict/filter before you build**

- **Generate** — [[diffusion-generative-design]]: [[watson-2023-rfdiffusion]] (fine-tuned RoseTTAFold) and, independently, [[ingraham-2023-chroma]] (bespoke polymer-aware diffusion) sample novel, steerable backbones.
- **Sequence** — [[sequence-design]]: [[dauparas-2022-proteinmpnn]] assigns a foldable sequence to a backbone in seconds, displacing Rosetta.
- **Filter** — [[structure-prediction]]: [[jumper-2021-alphafold2]] confidence scores decide, *in silico*, which designs are worth synthesizing.

[[bennett-2023-binder-design-dl]] shows the payoff concretely: dropping ProteinMPNN + AF2 filtering into the older Rosetta binder workflow raised success ~10×. [[de-novo-binder-design]] is the clearest application of the whole stack, and is now a chapter.

## How the pipeline was built and where it's going (batch 2)

- **Precursors.** Before diffusion, the same goals were met by **network hallucination** and **inpainting**. [[anishchenko-2021-hallucination]] established the method — invert a structure predictor by optimizing a random sequence until the network's own prediction sharpens — and [[wang-2022-functional-site-scaffolding]] ([[motif-scaffolding]]) and [[wicky-2022-symmetric-assembly-design]] ([[symmetric-assembly-design]]) are the same engine under two additional loss terms. RFdiffusion later absorbed all of it as conditional-diffusion tasks: what was a bespoke objective becomes a conditioning input.
- **Symmetric assemblies are now a chapter.** [[symmetric-oligomer-design]] traces the longest continuous arc in the wiki, from [[harbury-1993-coiled-coil-oligomer-switch]] and the fusion strategy of [[yeates-2002-supramolecular-assemblies]], through the parametric era ([[huang-2014-parametric-helical-bundles]], [[thomson-2014-alpha-helical-barrels]], [[boyken-2016-hydrogen-bond-network-oligomers]], [[courbet-2022-axle-rotor-assemblies]]) and its repeat-protein basis ([[brunette-2015-repeat-protein-universe]], [[wood-2018-ccbuilder]]), to hallucination, conditional diffusion, and back to gradient-based hallucination ([[frank-2024-relaxed-sequence-optimization]]).
- **Hallucination is not superseded.** The 2023 consensus that diffusion had displaced predictor inversion did not hold: [[frank-2024-relaxed-sequence-optimization]] fixed hallucination's two weaknesses — slow random search, and gradient descent in a discrete sequence space — by optimizing in a **relaxed** continuous sequence space, and reaches 1000-residue single chains (median TM 0.89) where [[watson-2023-rfdiffusion]] struggles past ~600. The two lineages now look complementary rather than sequential.
- **All-atom is the current frontier**, pursued by ≥3 distinct routes: atomize-in-predictor ([[krishna-2024-rosettafold-all-atom]]), intrinsic side-chain **superposition** ([[chu-2024-protpardelle]], Huang lab), and efficient **token+atom** generation ([[butcher-2025-rfdiffusion3]], ~10× cheaper, DNA binders). This mirrors, on the design side, AF3's all-atom move on the prediction side.
- **Synthetic structures as training data is now a settled bet.** [[lin-2024-genie2]] was "the first protein diffusion model to train on AFDB", replacing ~20,000 PDB structures of usable length with 588,570 FoldSeek-clustered AlphaFold predictions at pLDDT > 80 — moving diversity from 0.642 to 0.91 and PDB novelty from 0.041 to 0.41 relative to its predecessor, at designability matched with RFdiffusion. [[lin-2023-genie]] had already observed that "increasing training set size markedly improves Genie's sampling quality", and [[didi-2026-complexa-scaling]] makes the same bet for *complexes* with its synthetic Teddymer dataset built from domain–domain interactions of predicted monomers. Three sources, one direction. The open question the wiki cannot answer is what the ceiling of predicted-structure training is, since [[lin-2026-genie3]] uses **no experimentally determined monomeric structures at all**.
- **Sequence design is converging on codesign.** [[dauparas-2022-proteinmpnn]] and the learned-potential [[anand-2022-learned-potential-seqdesign]] (Huang lab) both showed learned energies beat Rosetta; Protpardelle now folds sequence *into* generation.
- **De novo antibodies now exist, at a measurable cost.** [[bennett-2026-de-novo-antibodies]] designs epitope-specific VHHs, scFvs and full IgGs from a **fine-tuned RFdiffusion2**, supplying the framework global-frame-invariantly so the antibody's rigid-body placement against the epitope is designed rather than assumed. Cryo-EM confirms **all six CDR loops** for one scFv and a 0.8 Å CDR3 for a VHH. The price of the format is explicit: **0–2% success per target**, against 19% for hotspot-conditioned miniprotein design from the same lab ([[watson-2023-rfdiffusion]]). The wiki's reading is that de novo design has displaced stage 1 of antibody discovery — epitope-specific generation, previously done by immunization followed by epitope mapping — and not stage 2, since OrthoRep maturation still supplies the final two orders of magnitude.
- **Applications reach harder targets.** [[vasquez-torres-2023-helical-peptide-binders]] extends [[de-novo-binder-design]] to floppy peptide hormones via **partial diffusion**, reaching picomolar affinity, and turns binders into biosensors and serum mass-spec reagents. [[stark-2025-boltzgen]] pushes the same frontier to intrinsically disordered regions and to small molecules.

## Binder design is now a chapter (batch 5)

- **The progression is a story of deletions.** [[de-novo-binder-design]] organises the field by what each generation stopped asking the designer to supply: the **binding mode** (grafting required a natural interaction to copy; hotspot conditioning removed that), then the **scaffold** ([[watson-2023-rfdiffusion]]), then the **chemistry** ([[krishna-2024-rosettafold-all-atom]], [[butcher-2025-rfdiffusion3]]), then the **modality** ([[stark-2025-boltzgen]]). The **epitope** has survived every generation as a manual input.
- **Hot spots finally have a source.** [[halperin-2004-interface-hot-spots]] supplies what the wiki had been using without defining: a hot spot is an alanine-scanning ΔΔG ≥ 1.9 kcal/mol residue, hot spots carry 4.2 cross-interface neighbours against 2.3 for other interface residues, and occupied volume around a residue correlates with its ΔΔG at **r² = 0.94**. That is why hotspot conditioning is a coherent instruction: it points a generator at the densely packed region where binding energy concentrates.
- **There is an independent lineage, and it works.** [[gainza-2020-masif]] and [[gainza-2023-surface-fingerprint-binders]] design binders by matching learned descriptors of molecular *surfaces* and grafting the retrieved seed — the inverse of telling a generator where to bind — reaching nanomolar affinities on spike, PD-L1 and CTLA-4 with crystal and cryo-EM confirmation, and a 4.2 µM PD-1 binder with no experimental optimisation at all. Its evaluation splits are the strictest in the wiki. Until batch 5 the wiki's binder coverage was a single lab's account of the field presented as the field.
- **The generative/hallucination split has collapsed.** [[didi-2026-complexa-scaling]] argues the dichotomy is false — generative methods are pure training-time optimisation, hallucination pure inference-time optimisation with no prior — and supplies both halves, beating [[bindcraft]] under *normalised compute budgets*. [[cho-2025-protein-hunter]] reaches the same place with no training at all, by letting a diffusion predictor's own hallucination be the prior. The wiki's reading is that hallucination's recurring adversarial-overfitting failure (see [[wicky-2022-symmetric-assembly-design]]) was never about the optimiser but about the absence of a prior to optimise inside — though no source runs the controlled experiment.
- **The architectural consensus is less settled than it looked.** Two moves the field made in 2024–25 — abandon SE(3)-equivariance to get all-atom detail, and replace generation with hallucination to get hit rates — are both reversed by [[lin-2026-genie3]], which keeps equivariance via a **branched-polymer** representation with partial atomization, runs 10× faster by moderating the reverse diffusion through a direction scale rather than a noise scale, and still beats the hallucination state of the art ([[bindcraft]]) on 7 of 10 benchmark binder problems, by a wider margin once runtime is normalised. Its blind-competition result is the strongest wet-lab datapoint in the batch: a **92 nM** binder to Nipah Glycoprotein G at a **1/8** hit rate, against aggregate 3/60, 1/100 and 2/288 for RFdiffusion, BindCraft and BoltzGen in the same challenge. The wiki's reading is that the consensus was adopted faster than it was tested.
- **The oracle's precision now has a number, and it is 12%.** [[lin-2026-genie3]] reports maximum 12% precision across its binder evaluation pipelines with complete failure on three of eleven targets, and says plainly that this "place[s] a low ceiling on the efficiency of binder design using *in silico* oracles as filters". It also caught designs passing an AlphaFold-Multimer benchmark while binding **off-target**, and repaired the filter by requiring ≥80% of specified hotspots within 5 Å — the published counterpart of the gate pathologies [[ruijgrok-2026-binder-pipeline-comparison]] measured operationally.
- **The filter, not the generator, sets the hit rate.** Three independent demonstrations: inserting the AF2 filter raised success ~10× with the generator unchanged ([[bennett-2023-binder-design-dl]]); an earlier BoltzGen whose ranking was close to random produced **no hits at all** from a substantially identical generator ([[stark-2025-boltzgen]]); and the generate-to-test ratio is **60,000:15** ([[stark-2025-boltzgen]]). Every published binder success rate is a rate measured after a filter of roughly that severity.

## Enzyme design is now a chapter (batch 7)

- **The field now has an independent enzyme-design lineage, and it reaches higher activities.** [[braun-2026-riff-diff]] (Oberdorfer lab) builds *on* [[watson-2023-rfdiffusion]] rather than retraining it, enumerating **artificial motif libraries** by rotamer inversion and enforcing a substrate pocket with an **α-helix placeholder that is deleted after diffusion** — because RFdiffusion's own substrate potential "failed to promote the formation of well-defined substrate binding pockets". From the same RA95.5-8F retroaldolase theozyme that [[ahern-2025-rfdiffusion2]] used, it reaches **290 M⁻¹ s⁻¹ against an extensively evolved enzyme's 320**, with **99% enantiomeric excess**, from 36 designs rather than 96 — roughly **45× the catalytic efficiency**. The wiki's reading is that this reframes §5's activity ceiling as a property of particular pipelines rather than of de novo design.
- **Preorganization has now been filtered prospectively, once.** [[kim-2026-metallohydrolases]] selected on **PLACER** ensembles, ranked its best design (53,000 M⁻¹ s⁻¹, against 3–60 for prior designed metallohydrolases) first — and then found that **seven designs from the same backbone family, rejected for suboptimal PLACER metrics, had very low or no activity**. Same campaign, same backbone, separated by an ensemble score. Taken with the productive-conformation correlation in [[choi-2026-cysteine-proteases]], the ensemble is now demonstrably the filterable quantity.
- **The amide barrier fell three times in one year.** Peptide bonds have half-lives of hundreds of years in neutral water ([[choi-2026-cysteine-proteases]]), which is why de novo enzyme design had been "largely limited to low-energy-barrier model reactions". Three 2026 campaigns cleared it by three chemistries: an activated **cysteine** nucleophile at 13/69 = 19% with rate enhancements to 3 × 10⁷ ([[choi-2026-cysteine-proteases]]); a water-activating **zinc** centre at 36% of 135, reaching >10⁸-fold and >10¹⁰ after four substitutions ([[chen-2026-metalloproteases]]); and **free-atom diffusion** delivering zero-shot phosphotriesterases and transthyretin-cleaving proteases at 56/96 in cis ([[butcher-2026-rfdiffusion3-science]]).
- **The remaining gap is preorganization, and everyone says so.** Crystal structures confirm the active sites were built as designed — Cα r.m.s.d. < 1.2 Å across six structures, 0.82–1.35 Å elsewhere — while efficiencies stay **10³–10⁴-fold below native** ([[chen-2026-metalloproteases]]). The mechanism is ensemble, not geometry: PLACER ensembles across the catalytic cycle show a non-productive His rotamer suppressed by one substitution, with the **fraction of productive conformations tracking activity** across a design series ([[choi-2026-cysteine-proteases]]). The wiki's reading is that the apparatus optimises a single structure while catalysis depends on a distribution.
- **What you model is what you get.** The largest single effect on outcome anywhere in the wiki comes from modelling the **catalytic water** in the filtered enzyme–substrate complex: 3/89 versus 31/38 one-hour hit rate, same generator, same designers, **unchanged numerical thresholds** ([[chen-2026-metalloproteases]], [[chen-2026-metalloproteases-supp]]). The converse is equally sharp — AlphaFold3 gave pLDDT **86.55** to a metal in a site with one coordinating ligand that binds no zinc, a design its authors note "would have been classified as a success" by standard filtering ([[elnesr-2026-zero-shot-metalloenzyme]]).
- **A second hypothesis about where catalysis comes from.** Every Baker-lab route transplants a native catalytic motif. [[elnesr-2026-zero-shot-metalloenzyme]] argues motif geometry alone "does not guarantee catalytic competence" because second-shell residues mediate reactivity, and optimises the **coordination sphere** directly by multi-objective evolutionary search over LigandMPNN and [[durr-2023-metal3d]]. It arrived at a **binuclear zinc site nature also uses, without being shown one**, at a rate enhancement of 3 × 10¹³ — the highest reported for any de novo hydrolase.
- **Training-set artefacts surface only when a predictor is used generatively.** Metal3D's corpus contains zinc sites with fewer than three coordinating residues that likely reflect crystallisation additives; its own supplement stratifies nearly every metric by coordination number and occupancy ([[durr-2023-metal3d-supp]]). That was a reporting detail until a design pipeline started optimising against it ([[elnesr-2026-zero-shot-metalloenzyme]]). The same pattern as the interface prior in [[anishchenko-2021-hallucination]], in a different modality.

## Cross-cutting observations

- **Prediction ⇄ design convergence.** Prediction models are reused as design oracles (AF2 filtering) and as generators (RoseTTAFold → RFdiffusion). Meanwhile prediction itself adopted diffusion in [[abramson-2024-alphafold3]]. The two problems are merging onto shared machinery.
- **Independent replication of the paradigm, now three ways.** RFdiffusion (Baker lab), Chroma (Generate Biomedicines) and Genie ([[lin-2023-genie]], AlQuraishi lab) arrived at diffusion-based design separately and by three different mechanisms — fine-tuning a predictor, a bespoke correlated-noise prior, and a forward/reverse representational asymmetry over oriented reference frames. Taken together the wiki reads this as a sign the approach is robust rather than a single group's trick. Genie is the only one of the three that is SE(3)-equivariant end to end, and it did it at 4.1 M parameters against RFdiffusion's 59.8 M ([[lin-2023-genie]]).
- **Validation discipline is the real unlock.** The recurring lesson across sources is not just better generators but cheap, reliable *in silico* filtering that collapses wet-lab search space.
- **But the oracle is now the thing to be suspicious of.** Taken together, batch 5 gives four distinct ways an *in silico* filter misleads: **adversarial overfitting** when it becomes an objective rather than a gate ([[wicky-2022-symmetric-assembly-design]]); **circularity** when generator and scorer share weights, which is a structural consequence of the unify-design-and-prediction direction both [[stark-2025-boltzgen]] and [[didi-2026-complexa-scaling]] take, and which [[ruijgrok-2026-binder-pipeline-comparison]] handles by declaring a scoring tier circular and promoting an independent predictor; **gates that deplete the signal they select for**, measured in that report as an AF2 gate reducing epitope contact and a promotion threshold discarding on-epitope designs; and **plain disagreement with experiment**, where [[gainza-2023-surface-fingerprint-binders]] confirmed designed interfaces by competition and mutagenesis for several binders whose AlphaFold predictions did *not* match the design models.
- **Atomic representation per pipeline stage is a validity condition, not a software detail.** [[ruijgrok-2026-binder-pipeline-comparison]] audits which stages can see sidechains and finds interface numbers being read off a Cα-only file in one pipeline and a generator's sidechains discarded before scoring in another. Given that interface energetics are a packing phenomenon correlating with occupied volume at r² = 0.94 ([[halperin-2004-interface-hot-spots]]), a backbone-only stage cannot evaluate the quantity that matters.
- **Conditioning moved from the coordinates into the sampling process.** Symmetric design is the cleanest place to watch this happen: symmetry was first *built into* a coordinate generator ([[huang-2014-parametric-helical-bundles-supp]]), then *rewarded* in a loss against a predictor ([[wicky-2022-symmetric-assembly-design]]), then *projected* onto the state at every denoising step ([[watson-2023-rfdiffusion-supp]]). The wiki's reading, developed in [[symmetric-oligomer-design]], is that this shift traded a geometry bottleneck for an interface-quality bottleneck — coverage improved far faster than yield.
- **The pre-deep-learning literature is doing real work.** Batch 3 showed that the wiki's synthesis was resting on Baker-lab-2022-onward sources alone for a problem with a thirty-year history. Where a chapter's claims trace only to recent papers, that is worth treating as a gap rather than a summary.
- **Generative models inherit priors nobody asked for.** [[anishchenko-2021-hallucination]] modelled every design as a monomer and got homo-oligomers and soluble aggregates anyway, because trRosetta had learned the sequence signatures of native protein–protein interfaces and did not disfavour surface hydrophobics enough. The same bias shows up as adversarial surfaces in [[wicky-2022-symmetric-assembly-design]], and again in [[frank-2024-relaxed-sequence-optimization]], whose designs formed homo-oligomers whenever the explicit negative-design filter against them was omitted. Three independent sightings across three years. The wiki's reading is that a predictor-derived generator carries its training distribution's interface statistics into every design — self-association is the default output, and every symmetric-design method is a way of *aiming* a propensity that is already there. See [[symmetric-oligomer-design]] §5.1 and §10.
